## A little more than a diary. A little more like you.

Some memories deserve more than a camera roll.

The blurry pictures with your best friend. A birthday that felt different. A song you couldn't stop listening to. The little details of an ordinary day that somehow became unforgettable.

**Memora is being built to give those moments a place of their own.**

Inspired by the charm of physical diaries, Pinterest-style scrapbooks, handwritten notes, Polaroid photographs, and the creative world of Gen Z, Memora is a digital space where people can create their own beautiful, personalized diary books.

Choose a cover. Fill the pages with your own photographs. Add stickers, ribbons, doodles, little notes, and meaningful details. Arrange everything in your own style and turn your everyday life into a book worth revisiting.

Not another place to scroll through memories.

A place to create something with them. ♡

## ✿ The vision

Memora brings the feeling of decorating a physical diary into a flexible digital creative studio.

Think of the creative freedom of Canva, the personal feeling of a handwritten journal, and the layered beauty of a scrapbook — brought together in one experience.

The goal is to make the entire process accessible, even to someone who has never used a design tool before.

No complicated software. No need to be a designer. No requirement to start with a blank page.

Just your memories, your imagination, and a diary that feels like yours.

**Memora is a real product in active development, intended for real users and a future public launch. This repository documents the ongoing development of that product, not a UI showcase or a throwaway prototype.**

## 🎀 Made for the way Gen Z expresses itself

Memora is being designed for students, teenagers, young creatives, and anyone who loves expressing their life visually.

Imagine creating a diary that feels completely different from everyone else's.

♡ A pink coquette journal filled with bows, hearts, and little love notes.

✦ A blue Y2K scrapbook filled with Polaroids, stars, and nostalgic details.

☁ A soft, minimalist diary for everyday thoughts and quiet moments.

🎧 A personal book dedicated to your favorite artists, music, books, or fandoms.

✈ A travel journal filled with real photographs, tickets, and little stories from every trip.

These are starting points, not limitations. The creative direction belongs to the person making the diary.

## ✧ What we're building

Memora is being developed as a genuine, interactive design application. The features below describe the product vision and ongoing roadmap; availability will be updated as each feature is implemented and tested.

### 01 · Your own diary, from cover to cover

Create a complete book instead of designing disconnected pages.

- Personalize covers, titles, colors, typography, and decorative details.
- Start with a template or a blank book.
- Create, duplicate, reorder, and remove pages.
- Move between pages in a natural book-like experience.
- Explore an immersive reading mode with page-turn animations.

### 02 · Your photographs, your memories

The heart of Memora is real life, not artificial representations of it.

- Upload photographs directly from your phone or computer.
- Place pictures inside Polaroid frames, collages, and scrapbook layouts.
- Resize, rotate, reposition, and layer photographs.
- Replace template pictures with your own moments.
- Arrange pictures alongside notes, captions, dates, and other memories.

Your photographs should remain your photographs. The tools exist to help you express them creatively.

### 03 · A little corner of creative freedom

A scrapbook is made special by the little things.

- Hearts, stars, flowers, ribbons, bows, and decorative badges.
- Washi tape, photo frames, paper cutouts, and borders.
- Arrows, doodles, speech bubbles, and simple shapes.
- Editable text, handwriting-inspired fonts, colors, and alignment.
- Backgrounds, paper textures, and layered decorative elements.

Every element is intended to be independently editable. Move a sticker without moving a photograph. Change a note without rebuilding the page. Make the design yours.

### 04 · Templates that are only the beginning

A template should help you start creating, not decide how your finished diary must look.

Memora aims to offer reusable, editable layouts for:

- Everyday diaries and personal reflections.
- Friendship books and meaningful gifts.
- Birthdays, anniversaries, and celebrations.
- Travel journals and adventures.
- Music, fandoms, hobbies, and favorite things.
- Vintage, pastel, minimalist, Y2K, coquette, and other aesthetics.

Users should be able to replace pictures, edit text, change colors, remove decorations, and rearrange the entire composition.

### 05 · A book worth keeping

A diary is meaningful because you can return to it.

The product roadmap includes:

- Reliable saving and recovery of creative work.
- Private user accounts and cloud-based book storage.
- PDF export for keeping or printing finished books.
- Image export for sharing selected pages.
- Optional read-only links and personalized digital gifts.
- Privacy controls that let users decide what remains personal and what they share.

These features will be developed with attention to reliability, security, and the real needs of the people using the product.

## ♡ Built around people, not just features

Memora is guided by a few simple beliefs.

**Personal expression comes first.** Everyone should have the freedom to create something that feels like them.

**The memories are real.** Users bring their own photographs, stories, and experiences. The application provides the creative tools.

**Beautiful shouldn't mean complicated.** A beginner should be able to make a lovely page without learning professional design software.

**The core experience should be accessible.** The long-term vision is to keep diary creation free and approachable, while exploring sustainable ways to support the product.

**Privacy is part of the experience.** Personal diaries should be private by default, with clear sharing choices and responsible handling of photographs and personal writing.

**Working software matters.** Every feature should be implemented, tested, and improved rather than presented as a visual placeholder.

## 🛠 The technology behind Memora

The application is being developed with a modern, maintainable web technology stack.

| Technology | Purpose |
|---|---|
| React | Interactive application interface |
| TypeScript | Safer, more maintainable code |
| Tailwind CSS | Consistent and responsive styling |
| Canvas editing tools | Manipulating photographs, text, and decorative elements |
| Framer Motion | Interface transitions and animation |
| Supabase or an equivalent backend | Planned authentication, database, and file storage |
| Git and GitHub | Version control and collaboration |

The final implementation will evolve as the editor becomes more sophisticated. Technology choices will be guided by functionality, reliability, performance, and sustainable operating costs.

AI-assisted development is part of the building process, but the product itself must work as a real application. Ordinary editing actions should not depend on expensive AI requests.

## 🚧 Development status

**Active development · Early stage**

Memora is currently being developed step by step, with an approximately six-month initial development horizon.

The focus is on building a reliable foundation before expanding the product.

**The roadmap**

- **Foundation** — Establish the application structure and core experience.
- **The editor** — Build working selection, movement, resizing, rotation, text, image uploads, and element controls.
- **Templates and assets** — Develop editable page layouts, covers, and a reusable decorative library.
- **The book experience** — Connect multiple pages, custom covers, and reading mode.
- **Saving and accounts** — Introduce reliable persistence, secure storage, and private user accounts.
- **Export and sharing** — Build ways to preserve, download, and selectively share finished diaries.
- **Testing and launch preparation** — Improve responsiveness, accessibility, security, performance, and reliability.

This is a development roadmap, not a declaration that every feature is already available. Completed features will be documented as they become functional and are tested.

The intention is to build carefully, learn from real feedback, and launch when the product is ready.
